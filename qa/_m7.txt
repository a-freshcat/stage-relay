0.1.7: the stage-summary path is actually reachable, and a supervisor who is also the reporter can submit

Codex reviewed 0.1.5 and was right about two severed links that 0.1.6 had not
closed. Both were real.

The supervisor could not reach the request at all. `summary_request` was missing
from `AGENT_ACTIONS`, so the bridge answered "members cannot perform user
decisions" before the store ever saw it — the MCP tool existed and nothing behind
it was reachable. It is now in that set; the store still enforces the role.

A supervisor who is also the reporter deadlocked. The request was correctly
refused with "submit directly", and the submit path then demanded a prior request
that could never exist. Submitting on your own authority is now allowed for
exactly that case, once, and recorded as `self_submitted` so it is not mistaken
for a user-dispatched summary.

Stage scope is carried end to end: `issue_id` selects the stage, the entry is
persisted with a revision, a second submission on the same revision is refused,
and a different stage keeps its own history instead of overwriting `p.summary`.

Verified through the real MCP bridge on the deployed build, not just in tests:
supervisor requests a stage over `relay_request_summary`, the reply carries the
issue id and revision 1, the reporter submits over `relay_stage_summary`, the
stage is persisted as submitted, and a repeat submission is refused. Fixture
projects were closed and recycled through the normal flow; the real project,
its bindings and its chat are unchanged.

Tests: 164 run, 1 skipped.
